    How to play

    Building a word

    • Letters must touch each other: horizontally, vertically or diagonally.
    • A square can only be used once in a given word.
    • Four letters minimum.
    • With a keyboard: type the word, then Enter. With a mouse or a finger: tap the letters one by one, or drag across them.

    Which words count

    • English dictionary words, and those only.
    • Every form counts: plurals, conjugations, participles. walk, walks, walked and walking are all valid.
    • No proper nouns.

    Scoring

    4 letters = 1 point · 5 = 2 · 6 = 3 · 7 = 5 · 8 or more = 11. Every grid hides at least one eight-letter word — find it.

    Today’s grid

    It is derived from the date: everyone plays the same grid on the same day, and it changes at midnight. Nothing is sent over the Internet, your game stays on this device.
